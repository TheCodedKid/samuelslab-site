# Custom Casio Watches — Film Mods

The lowest-effort, highest-vibes Casio mod: drop a tinted film between the glass and the LCD and suddenly your $15 F91W looks like a different watch. No glue, no soldering, fully reversible.

## What the film actually does

The film is a thin, partially-transparent color sheet that sits on top of (or just under) the crystal, in front of the LCD. It tints the whole face — background, segments, backlight — without touching the polarizer underneath. Pop the back off, slide it in, screw the back on. Done in 10 minutes if you don't lose a screw in the carpet.

Common colors floating around on Etsy / Amazon / AliExpress:

- **Red** — angriest, very "tactical"
- **Blue** — the most popular one, clean and cool
- **Green** — CRT / military gauge vibe
- **Yellow / amber** — DW5030-style, my favorite look
- **Purple / orange / pink** — Miami-Vice-core
- **Gradient sheets** — fade from one color to another across the face

Multi-packs (40–80 filters, every color) run ~$8–15 and are way more film than one person needs.

## Models this works on

- **F91W** — the canonical victim, plenty of room behind the crystal
- **A158 / A168 / AE1200 ("Royale")** — same idea, slightly more careful disassembly
- **W59 / F105** — basically F91W internals

## How to do it (F91W, ~10 min)

1. Flip the watch, four small Phillips screws on the back, set them somewhere you won't lose them.
2. Lift the back off, lift the module out (it just sits in there).
3. Pop the metal cover off the LCD — gentle pry, don't bend it.
4. Cut the film to the size of the LCD window (just trace it). A fresh blade matters more than the scissors.
5. Lay the film over the LCD. Some people put it on top of the metal cover instead — both work, slightly different look.
6. Reassemble in reverse. Don't overtighten the screws or you'll strip the plastic posts.

Wear gloves or you'll get fingerprints on the inside of the crystal and hate yourself.

## Related, deeper mods

If film isn't enough:

- **Polarizer rotation / negative display** — pull the polarizing film off the LCD, rotate 90°, reapply. Inverts the display (segments light, background dark).
- **Translucent LCD** — strip the rear reflector entirely so you can see the module guts behind the digits. Looks incredible, breaks legibility.
- **Bezel swap, band swap, text removal** — separate rabbit holes, see the [[Project Ideas/Custom Casio Watches Guide|Custom Casio Watches Guide]].

## Where to buy films

- Etsy — search "F91W color filter" — small sellers, ships fast
- Amazon — LUAATT and NZELSW both sell 40–80 packs of pre-cut F91W filters
- eBay UK — 10-pack "color screen modification" sets
- AliExpress — cheapest if you can wait
