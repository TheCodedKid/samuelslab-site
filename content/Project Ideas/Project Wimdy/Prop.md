## Material

There are a couple different materials that we can go with for the turbine propeller. Keep in mind that the prop design itself is going to be heavily based on a HAWT system (although, vertical would be interesting).

### Carbon Fiber

Pros :

- Lightweight
- Readily Available
- Durable

Cons :

- Will shatter on strong impact
- Weakens in hotter climate or long time heat exposure
- Expensive

### Wood

Pros :

- Less expensive
- More Flexible
- Can be lightweight with proper material
