## Mock Up

![[Media/6E6F7615-A3F2-4FE2-980A-83E6F4592E00.jpeg]]

The basic mock up is rather simple in nature, but the biggest thing is the weight distribution across the frame. The idea is everything has to be relatively balanced across both axis of the frame. This system needs to be light, hopefully the majority of the mass coming from the plastic motor.

  

## Segmentation

The biggest thing that would make this system super recoverable while also being easier to build has to do with the entire inflatable frame being made of segments.
