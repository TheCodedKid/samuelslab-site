## Nano Tank Build

This is the real build now. I'm doing a nano, and I've actually started picking up parts instead of just planning.

**Tank** — I scored a [UNS Dual All-in-One](https://shop.glassaqua.com/products/uns-dual-all-in-one-glass-aquarium-tank) off Facebook Marketplace for $60, which is a steal. It's the core of the whole thing, so everything else gets scaled to its footprint.

**Lighting** — Still going with the [Twinstar entry-level](https://twinstarstore.kr/collections/entry-level-lighting) light. It's budget, but for the low/medium plants I'm after it's plenty.

**Hardscape** — Went a different route than the dragon stone I had planned. Hit up a local rock landscape lot and grabbed some Indian blue slate, plus a lava rock out of their free bin. Both are inert so they won't mess with my water params, and the slate should give me some nice layered structure to work with.

**Substrate** — Still leaning toward UNS Controsoil. It's an active aquasoil, buffers pH/KH down, and it's good for plants and shrimp. Just need to decide normal vs powder — powder if I want a finer foreground.

**Plants** — Now that the scape is coming together I'm shopping [Glass Aqua's aquatic plants](https://shop.glassaqua.com/collections/aquatic-plants). Still narrowing down what I want, notes to come.
