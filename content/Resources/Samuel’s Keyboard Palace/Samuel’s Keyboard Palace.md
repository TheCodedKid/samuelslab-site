## My Work

### [ADBee](https://github.com/TheCodedKid/adbee)
ADB → USB converter for old Apple keyboards/mice. XIAO ESP32-S3 reads the single-wire ADB bus and shows up as USB HID. Mini-DIN 4-pin in, BSS138 level shift, TVS for ESD. See [[Project Ideas/ADBee|ADBee]].

## Want to Look Into

### [Bauer Lite DynaPak Plus](https://omnitype.com/products/bauer-lite-dynapak-plus)
Electrocapacitive (Topre-style) conversion for the Bauer Lite using DynaCap switches (Clever Keebs). MX keycap compatible. Budget route to the feel of the [Norbauer Seneca](https://www.norbauer.co/pages/the-seneca), which uses Norbauer's own EC switches.

### Seneca boot-up tone
Seneca plays a vintage-computer chime on boot (different tone for Mac vs Win/Linux mode). Want something like this in my own boards. Related: [des-pas](https://github.com/TheCodedKid/des-pas) (my NixOS login sound).
