## Intro

[crabeye](https://github.com/TheCodedKid/crabeye) is a TUI [RSVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_serial_visual_presentation) reader written in Rust. RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) flashes one word at a time in a fixed spot so your eyes don't move — a speed-reading trick, in the terminal.

Built on [Ratatui](https://ratatui.rs). Early days / WIP.

## The cheese

Scaffolded from the Ratatui hello-world template. The plan: feed it text, pick a words-per-minute rate, and let it flash the stream word by word.
