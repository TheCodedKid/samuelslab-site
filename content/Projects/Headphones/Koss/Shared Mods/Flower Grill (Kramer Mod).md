## Description

Custom flower grill (Kramer mod) for Koss headphones — fits Porta Pro, KPH40, and KSC75. Per the designer, it improves the bass on the KSC75.

Source: [r/kossmods post](https://www.reddit.com/r/kossmods/comments/1t61d5b/2pin_mmcx_and_flower_grill_mods_for_all_popular/)
