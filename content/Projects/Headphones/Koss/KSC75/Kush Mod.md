## Thingiverse Description

This Mod takes the KSC75 and upgrades the comfort for those looking to stretch the limits of their Koss experience.

Yaxi S-Cushion:

[https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B089SST2YM/](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B089SST2YM/)

Geekria Pads:

[https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0155XZJ4I/](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0155XZJ4I/)

Installation is as easy as pressing the KSC into place but does require a headband as the ear clips will not fit with this mod. There are several options for headbands:

PE Headband:

[https://www.amazon.com/Parts-Express-Stereo-Lightweight-Headphones/dp/B0002KQZJS/](https://www.amazon.com/Parts-Express-Stereo-Lightweight-Headphones/dp/B0002KQZJS/)

Retrospekt Headband:

[https://retrospekt.com/products/koss-p-21-retro-foam-on-ear-headphones](https://retrospekt.com/products/koss-p-21-retro-foam-on-ear-headphones)

3D Printed headband by Dovakiiin:

[https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5150982](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5150982)

[The (froggy) version is compatible with both the 3.5mm and the mmcx Froggie Mods]

If you need help with printing? Have questions about the desings? Want to keep up with what we're prototyping? Join our discord [https://discord.gg/fb4HdDvErF](https://discord.gg/fb4HdDvErF)

Print Settings

Supports:

Yes

Notes:

Increase your wall count until there is no air between the walls for the best experience

Print the part at a 5 degree angle so the surface the driver contacts with is one layer and flat to ensure a good seal
