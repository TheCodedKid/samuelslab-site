## Description

Improved MMCX connector mod for the Koss KSC75. An updated take on the existing MMCX adapters (see [[Projects/Headphones/Koss/KSC75/KSC75 MMCX Adapter|KSC75 MMCX Adapter]] and [[Projects/Headphones/Koss/KSC75/KSC75 Froggie Mod (MMCX)|KSC75 Froggie Mod (MMCX)]]) with refined geometry.

Source: [r/kossmods post](https://www.reddit.com/r/kossmods/comments/1t61d5b/2pin_mmcx_and_flower_grill_mods_for_all_popular/)
