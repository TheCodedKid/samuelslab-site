## Description

Improved 2-Pin (0.78mm) connector mod for the Koss KSC75. Replaces the stock cable attachment with a standard 2-Pin 0.78 socket so any compatible IEM/headphone cable can be used.

Source: [r/kossmods post](https://www.reddit.com/r/kossmods/comments/1t61d5b/2pin_mmcx_and_flower_grill_mods_for_all_popular/)
