## Description

Improved 2-Pin (0.78mm) connector mod for the Koss Porta Pro and KPH40. Adds a standard 2-Pin 0.78 socket for swappable cables.

Source: [r/kossmods post](https://www.reddit.com/r/kossmods/comments/1t61d5b/2pin_mmcx_and_flower_grill_mods_for_all_popular/)
