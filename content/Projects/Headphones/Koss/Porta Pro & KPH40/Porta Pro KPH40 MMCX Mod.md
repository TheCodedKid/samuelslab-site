## Description

Improved MMCX connector mod for the Koss Porta Pro and KPH40. Replaces the stock cable attachment with an MMCX socket for swappable cables.

Source: [r/kossmods post](https://www.reddit.com/r/kossmods/comments/1t61d5b/2pin_mmcx_and_flower_grill_mods_for_all_popular/)
