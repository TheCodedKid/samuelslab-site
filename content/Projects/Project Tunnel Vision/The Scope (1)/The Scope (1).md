## Function in Action

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTE4_WEJgTo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTE4_WEJgTo)

The watch’s main matrix tells you the hour value an on the horizontal axis in ascending order from left to right, whereas the minutes are told in the closest lower 5 minute interval. The “port” scope light on the top right tells you the additional minutes on top of the matrix value, ranging from one to four.

![[Media/Untitled 7 2.png|Untitled 7 2.png]]

The button basically calls for the time to be told, however it is use to setup the initial time as well.
