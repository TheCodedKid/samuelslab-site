OneWheel is a single-wheel, self-balancing electric board, and once you've ridden one for a while, you start to notice all the little things you wish were different. This repo is where I've been parking those ideas — designs, prototypes, and parts for OneWheel mods I've built or am building.

The repo lives at [TheCodedKid/OneWheel-Stuff](https://github.com/TheCodedKid/OneWheel-Stuff).

Current contents:

- **Float Pads** — replacement/upgrade foot pads.
- **OneChair** — a chair-style accessory project for the OneWheel.
- **OneWings** — wing-style add-ons.

For now, these are shared selectively with people who show real interest in the work. The longer-term plan is to clean up the designs enough to sell them, but until then it's a mix of hobbyist tinkering and learning what holds up under actual riding.
