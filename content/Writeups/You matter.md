I visit [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/news) pretty often, in fact, probably more so than any other news _anything_. I find it to be a good start to my days, or a good round off to my evenings. For me, it is a sense of escape from the pain of "normal news". Sometimes there is an odd one out that comes up here and their, but for the most part, all of articles posted shroud themselves in technical ambiance. It is comforting, and articles themselves get me thinking about new tools, lines of thinking, methods to better solutions, etc. 

![[Media/Pasted image 20251027223140.png]]
But this one was different for me.

The common understanding/desensitized knowledge of mental health is something that I think a good amount of people experience (and truthfully, can explain it better than I can).

![[Media/Pasted image 20251027224301.png]]  
_But one million people a week talk about it with a computer, the only thing that is willing to listen._

"The company says that 0.15% of ChatGPT’s active users in a given week have 'conversations that include explicit indicators of potential suicidal planning or intent.'" (Zeff)

As of earlier this year (2026), OpenAI reports to roughly [900 million weekly active users](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users/), with that, [24.8 percent are between the ages of 18-24 years old, whereas 28.8 percent are between 25-34](https://www.aiprm.com/openai-statistics/). 

Suicide is never something that is fun for me to talk about. I have lost good friends to it, witness the effect it has on those who loved/cared for them. For the possiblilty that a machine can save someone's troubled life, let alone the creators agknowledging this issue, is a win in my book.

Because I understand that reading when having these kinds of thoughts is hard, I will make this one short:

**You, what you do, who you are, and the passions and talents you bring to this world matter.**

**Remember to talk to someone about it, even if it means breaking out of the comfortablilty to get pass the machine.** 

**The risk of the machine telling you want you *might* want *at the time* is a real risk, so please be mindful of this very fact.** 

Talk to someone, and if you need a person, reach out. I am always around.
